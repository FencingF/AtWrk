## Final inspiration statement

Help college tactical athletes **see objective progress** across lifting, running, swimming, and recovery so they **train smarter, hit standards, and avoid overuse**.

## Solutions considered from preliminary ideation

* **Progress Graphs & Logbook** for multi-modal tracking with PRs and trends.
* **Benchmarking & Insights** against selection/fitness standards and anonymized cohort averages.
* **Habit & Recovery Tracker** with simple macros, sleep/soreness check-ins, and streaks.

## Final app idea 

**AtWrk — a workout progress web app** that:

* **Tracks** strength, runs, swims, and rucks with **clean graphs** showing improvement over time.
* **Integrates Strava** to auto-ingest runs/swims; manual logging for lifts/rucks.
* **Benchmarks** users and **anonymized cohort averages** to surface weak links and projected timelines.
* Adds **lightweight macros** and **daily recovery check-ins** to keep the plan realistic without feature bloat.
* Prioritizes **privacy/OPSEC**: opt-in sharing, no location auto-sharing, anonymized comparisons.
